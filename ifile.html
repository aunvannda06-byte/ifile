Frequently Asked Questions

Can I transfer files from my phone to a PC?

Yes. Any device with a modern browser works — phone to PC, PC to phone, or phone to phone.

Can I transfer between iPhone and Android?

Yes. Ifile runs in the browser, so it works on every platform.

Does Ifile require internet?

Yes. Devices connect over the internet by scanning a QR code.

What types of files can I transfer?

Photos, videos, documents, music, ZIP archives and more.

Is my data secure?

Transfers use an encrypted connection and files are not stored on the server.
