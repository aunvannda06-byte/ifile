Frequently Asked Questions

Can I transfer between iPhone and Android?

Yes. Ifile works in any modern browser on every platform.

Does Ifile require internet?

QR mode uses the internet. Bluetooth mode works between nearby devices without it.

What file types can I transfer?

Photos, videos, documents, music, ZIP archives, folders and more.

Is my data secure?

Transfers are encrypted and files are not kept longer than needed.
